Scan2PDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Scan2PDF converts photos into PDF files. All processing happens on your device. Your photos and PDFs are never uploaded to any server, and we do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Camera and photos

The app asks to use your camera or choose images from your gallery only so you can scan documents. Images stay on your device (in the app's local storage) until you delete them or tap "Clear".

No accounts, no tracking

The app has no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.
